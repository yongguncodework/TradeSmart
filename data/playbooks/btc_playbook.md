# Bitcoin (BTC-USD) Playbook

## Core thesis framework
- Trade BTC as a macro + momentum sleeve, not as a single indicator play.
- Prefer entries after volatility compression (narrow 5–10 day range) followed by expansion with volume.
- Align with ETF trend: if QQQ and SPY both lose 20-day structure, reduce BTC long exposure.

## Position sizing
- Default sleeve: 5–12% of portfolio depending on volatility regime.
- Cut size by 50% when daily ATR percentile is in the top 20% of the last 90 days.
- Never add to a losing BTC position more than once per week (no revenge scaling).

## Entry checklist
1. Higher-timeframe trend intact (price above 50-day for swing longs).
2. Pullback holds prior breakout zone or weekly VWAP reclaim.
3. Risk per trade <= 1% of portfolio with stop below structure invalidation.

## Exit rules
- Take partial profits at +2R; trail remainder with weekly low or ATR-based stop.
- Hard exit if BTC closes below 200-day while DXY breaks multi-month highs.

## Invalidation signals
- Failed breakout with volume spike and same-day reversal > 4%.
- Exchange/stablecoin stress headlines with simultaneous correlation spike to equities down day.

## Session notes
- US equity open often drives BTC direction on risk-on/risk-off days.
- Weekend gaps: reduce size before Sunday evening if CME gap risk is elevated.
