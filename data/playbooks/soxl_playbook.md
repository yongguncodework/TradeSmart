# SOXL (3x Semiconductors) Playbook

## Product awareness
- SOXL is a **3x leveraged daily-reset ETF** — path dependency and volatility decay matter.
- Intended for **short holding periods** (intraday to a few days), not buy-and-hold.
- SMH and QQQ trend should confirm before initiating SOXL swing trades.

## When SOXL is in play
- SMH breaks above consolidation with sector breadth improvement.
- NVDA/AMD leadership resumes after a clean pullback (not a vertical spike).
- VIX stable or falling; avoid fresh SOXL longs when VIX spikes > 20% in a day.

## Position sizing (strict)
- Max portfolio allocation: **8%** for any leveraged ETF sleeve.
- Default starter: 2–4% with add only on confirmation (break + hold).
- If SOXL daily move exceeds ±8%, **no new entries** until next session.

## Entry patterns
1. **Pullback continuation**: SOXL holds 20-day EMA while SMH makes higher low.
2. **Breakout retest**: prior range high reclaimed on volume; enter on retest hold.
3. **Mean reversion (advanced)**: only after capitulation wick + sector stabilization.

## Risk management
- Stop: below retest low or 1.5x average daily range — whichever is tighter logically.
- If down 1.5R intraday, exit — do not martingale leveraged products.
- Rotate to QQQ or cash when SOXL whipsaws three sessions in a row.

## Pair context
- Compare SOXL vs SMH relative strength; divergence warns of decay environment.
- Watch USD and rates headlines — semis are rate-sensitive.

## Red flags
- SOXL up but SMH flat/down (decay warning).
- Earnings cluster for top SMH weights within 48 hours — reduce size.
- Gap open > 5% without catalyst clarity — wait for first hour structure.
