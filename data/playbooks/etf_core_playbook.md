# ETF Core Sleeve Playbook (QQQ, SPY, SMH)

## Portfolio role
- ETFs provide the **core trend anchor** for the portfolio.
- QQQ = growth/tech exposure; SPY = broad market filter; SMH = semiconductor sector timing.

## Regime filter (weekly)
| Regime | SPY vs 200d | QQQ vs 200d | Action |
|--------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Risk-on | Above | Above | Full core sleeve allowed |
| Mixed | Above | Below | Reduce QQQ, favor SPY |
| Risk-off | Below | Below | Cut core exposure 50%+, raise cash |

## Entry discipline
- Add on weekly closes, not intraday noise.
- Require SPY and QQQ not to diverge sharply (> 3% 5-day spread) before sizing up.
- SMH adds only when sector breadth confirms (not single-name hype).

## Sizing guidelines
- SPY/QQQ combined core: up to 25–35% in risk-on regimes.
- Single ETF tranche: default 5–10% per add.
- Rebalance monthly or after ±15% drift from target weights.

## Correlation with BTC sleeve
- When BTC and QQQ correlate > 0.7 on 20-day window, treat as one risk bucket for sizing.
- Do not double-count exposure across BTC and QQQ during macro shock weeks.

## Exit / de-risk triggers
- SPY loses 50-day with rising volume distribution days.
- Credit spread widening + semis failing breakout (SMH rolls over).
- FOMC/CPI week: halve new ETF adds until event volatility clears.

## Review cadence
- Sunday: update regime table and next-week catalyst map.
- Daily: 5-minute scan of SPY, QQQ, SMH vs key moving averages only.
